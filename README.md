# Sean Tao · Growth, Brand & AI

I take products into new markets and make them grow. Over 12 years I have launched products in Korea, Japan, the US, India, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Hong Kong and Taiwan, built local teams from zero, worked with 500+ KOLs (online influencers), and co-founded three companies at a Hong Kong venture studio.

For the last two years I have built with AI hands-on. This portfolio shows how: to grow and market products faster, to make product decisions with evidence, and to research markets with discipline.

## Product

| Work | What it is |
|---|---|
