# Asia go-to-market: bringing Western companies into Asia

**Role:** Founding Partner, Everest Ventures Group (a Hong Kong venture studio) · 2018–2026

## The problem

Most "go global" stories are about Asian companies going West. This is the reverse.

Our venture studio backed many US and European companies. They wanted users in Asia, but Asia is not one market. Each country has its own language, its own platforms and its own trusted voices. A Western team cannot simply translate its ads and expect them to work.

## What I did

- **Made "Asia traffic" the core support we gave our portfolio companies.** In plain words: helping them get users and attention in Asia.
- **Combined two networks into one go-to-market engine:**
  - The studio's Asia network of **KOLs and KOCs**. KOLs are key opinion leaders (influencers). KOCs are key opinion consumers: everyday users whose word their friends trust.
  - **BlockTempo**, a crypto media brand in Taiwan.
- **Helped BlockTempo strengthen its position as a leading media brand in Taiwan,** so it could be a stronger channel for every company we worked with.
- **Opened doors across the region:** introductions to miners, family offices and exchanges in Asia.

## What I learned

- **Trust is local.** A Western brand gets heard in Asia faster through local voices people already follow than through its own ads.
- **Own a channel, not just a budget.** Having a media brand in the family meant we could tell a story, not only buy reach.
- **Reverse go-global uses the same skills as going global:** find the local niche, say it in local words, pick the local channels, and measure.
