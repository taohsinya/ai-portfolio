# Campaigns

Marketing work I have led over 12 years, before and alongside my work with AI. Each page follows the same shape: the problem, what I did, the result, and what I learned.

## How I approach marketing

1. **Find the niche.** Look for a group of people the big players are not serving well.
2. **Say it in one clear line.** If the message explains why you are different, every ad and post gets easier.
3. **Pick the right channels.** Local teams, influencers, communities, TV or bus ads: whatever that audience actually trusts.
4. **Measure.** Track a few numbers that matter, such as return on ad spend, cost per install or funded users, and move money to what works.
