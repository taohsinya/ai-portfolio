# OpenSocial: growing a developer and user community from zero

**Role:** Co-founder · 2023–2025

## The problem

OpenSocial is a set of building blocks for social apps on blockchains: feeds, chat rooms, communities ("tribes"), social graphs, advertising and more. Developers can put the pieces together to build their own social app quickly.

A toolkit like this has two audiences, and both start at zero:
- **Developers**, who need a reason to build on it.
- **Users**, who need apps worth using.

## What I did

**1. Showed what the toolkit could do by building with it.**
I led product growth. I designed a reusable **gamification layer** (points, badges, rewards and seasons) that could plug into OpenSocial's building blocks. On top of it the team built **three viral social apps**: SoMon, BreadnButter and Zeek. Each app was proof for developers that the toolkit worked.

**2. Weekly AMAs to get known by communities and influencers.**
AMA means "ask me anything", a live audio chat. The goal: get recognised by communities and influencers so we could bring them onto our products.
- Every week, a short 20–30 minute session with 2–3 guests, kept casual, about hot topics in social crypto.
- Guests were invited a week ahead, aligned on questions, and given a banner with their photo to share with their own followers.
- After each session we posted a recap and used polls to let the audience choose the next guest and topic.

**3. Product launch campaign: "create tribes".**
When we launched tribes (communities anyone can create), the plan was built around influencers and communities:
- A launch kit: demo video, screenshots and a one-pager.
- Outreach to a curated list of influencers and communities, with follow-up calls for the most important ones.
- A contest for the most popular new tribes, plus badges for early creators and top users.
- Launched in "seasons" so there was always a next reason to come back.
- Measured daily: new tribes, sign-ups by channel, and activity in each new community.

**4. "Before Sunset": an event at Asia Blockchain Summit 2024 in Taipei.**
A co-hosted evening event with **Pudgy Penguins Asia** and **Kaia** on 7 August 2024. It had short keynotes from all three partners, networking, a booth for our apps, a claw machine and a prize draw. Partnering with well-loved brands put us in front of their communities, not just ours.

**5. A plan for user-made content.**
To make the platform feel active, we planned themed community spaces on topics people already cared about (trading, memes, NFTs, macro markets), an ambassador programme that rewarded top contributors, and collaborations with respected projects and creators.

## Results

- Developer ecosystem grew from **0 to 80+** developers (30 in-house at the venture studio plus 50+ outside developers, as stated in the May 2024 public announcement).
- **Three viral social apps** built on one reusable gamification layer.
- Raised a **$5M seed round** in 2024, backed by a **$15M ecosystem fund** for developers.

## What I learned

- **For a platform, the best marketing is a product built on it.** The three apps were the most convincing pitch to developers.
- **Borrow audiences through partners.** Co-hosting with Pudgy Penguins Asia and Kaia, and featuring guests in AMAs, reached people we could not reach alone.
- **Give people a reason to come back.** Seasons, badges and contests turned a single launch into a series of moments.
