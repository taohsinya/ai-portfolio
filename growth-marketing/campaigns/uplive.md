**Role:** Global Marketing Lead, Asia Innovations Group · 2014–2017

## The problem

Uplive is a live-streaming app. When I joined, Chinese consumer apps were only starting to look abroad. We were part of China's first wave of apps going global, so there was no playbook to copy.

Every country was different: different habits, different media, different costs. A plan that worked in one market could lose money in the next.

## What I did

- **Built local teams on the ground.** I went to Taiwan, Hong Kong, Vietnam and Korea myself and set up a local team in each one. Local people know the local media, the local slang and what feels right.
- **Ran all of marketing, not just digital.** Online ads were the base. On top of that we ran TV commercials, metro ads and bus ads, so people saw the brand both on their phones and on their way to work.
- **Managed a large budget across many markets.** About US$10M a year, with a core team of 10.
- **Measured every market on return on ad spend (ROAS).** ROAS is the revenue you get back for each dollar you spend on ads. We moved money towards the markets that paid back and away from the ones that did not.

## Results

- Launched in **50+ countries**.
- Grew to about **30 million users** during my time.
- **ROAS of up to 150%** in the best markets: Taiwan, Hong Kong and the Middle East.
- **India was the weakest market** on ROAS.

## What I learned

- **One global plan does not work.** The same app, the same budget and the same ads gave very different returns in Taiwan and in India. You have to measure market by market.
- **Local teams pay for themselves.** Being on the ground in each key market was how we learned what each audience wanted.
- **Offline media still matters.** TV, metro and bus ads made the brand feel real and trusted, and they supported what we did online.
